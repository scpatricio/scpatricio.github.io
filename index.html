Silvio C. Patricio

Demographer and statistician

I study how mortality changes with age, how it differs across populations, and what those patterns tell us about human aging.

Postdoctoral researcher at the Interdisciplinary Centre on Population Dynamics, University of Southern Denmark.

Google Scholar ResearchGate LinkedIn

Featured research · PNAS, 2026

The rhythm of aging

My analysis finds little evidence of a long-term change in how fast individual mortality rises with age. The findings are consistent with people reaching the same mortality levels later in life.
